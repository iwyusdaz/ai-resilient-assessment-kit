# Educator review — invitation + form (human version)

The invitation email and the review form for real educator reviewers (≥ 3, ideally across disciplines). The agent-reviewed version of this form ran at build time and is labelled **Agent review**; when human educators complete it, results go to `docs/evaluation/educator-review.md` and every resulting change to `docs/evaluation/revisions.md`.

## Invitation text (adapt and send)

> **Subject: 45–60 minutes: pressure-test an AI-era assessment kit?**
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I've built an open kit that stress-tests three common university assignments (essay, problem set, research report) against generative AI and redesigns them with explicit AI-use levels, process evidence and new rubrics. It includes a decision tool and a ready-to-run 90-minute workshop.
>
> I'm recruiting three educators to pressure-test one case each. You'd take **[Case A/B/C]** plus the decision tool, and tell me where it breaks: clarity, feasibility, equity, and whether you'd actually use it. 45–60 minutes, entirely anonymous if you prefer, and I'll share the revision log so you see what your feedback changed.
>
> The kit: https://iwyusdaz.github.io/ai-resilient-assessment-kit/
>
> Interested? Reply and I'll send the form and the consent note (short, honest, one page).
>
> [Name]

## Review form

*(Same content as the published form in `docs/evaluation/educator-review.md`. Print or clone into a form tool. Anonymous completion supported — no name field.)*

### Profile (optional)

- Discipline/role: ______  ·  Years teaching: ______  ·  Typical class size: ______
- Have you already changed an assessment because of AI? yes / partly / no

### The case (1–5 + a paragraph each)

1. **Clarity** — could you follow stress-test → redesign → rubric without asking anyone?
2. **Validity** — does the redesign certify what the original claimed to certify?
3. **Feasibility** — do the grading-time and scaling notes survive YOUR timetable? What breaks first?
4. **Equity** — what does this do for/to multilingual students, students with accommodations, students without AI access?
5. **Student experience** — would you defend this to students in week 1?
6. **Would you use it?** as-is / with changes / no — the one change that matters most: ______

### Decision tool (10 minutes)

7. Run one of your own assessments through the six questions. Were the recommendation and its "Why this recommendation?" reasoning right / partly right / wrong? What reasoning is missing?
8. Anything missing from the eight approaches?

### Missing pieces

9. What would this kit need for YOUR context (discipline, institution type, country)?

### Permission

- [ ] You may quote my feedback anonymously in the kit's evaluation pages.
- [ ] You may paraphrase my feedback anonymously.
- [ ] Please do not quote or paraphrase me.
