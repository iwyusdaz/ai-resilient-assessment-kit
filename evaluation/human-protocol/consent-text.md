# Consent text — educator reviewers and workshop participants

Plain-language consent for the kit's human protocols (educator review, optional workshop delivery). Adapt to your institution's ethics guidance; if your context requires formal ethics approval, obtain it before collecting anything.

---

**You are being invited to review learning-design materials** (and, optionally, to attend a 90-minute workshop) **from the AI-Resilient Assessment Kit.** Before you agree, here is what participation involves. You can stop at any point, for any reason, without giving an explanation.

## What you would do

- Review one assignment case and the decision tool (about 45–60 minutes) using the review form, and/or participate in the 90-minute workshop.
- Give feedback anonymously if you prefer. You never have to attach your name.

## What we collect

- Your form responses, and any comments you choose to give on the redesign canvas.
- Nothing else: no names unless you choose to give one, no emails, no role details you don't volunteer, no analytics from the materials themselves (the decision tool stores nothing).

## How it is used

- To improve the kit: feedback is summarised, and accepted/rejected changes are logged in the kit's revisions page.
- Quotes are published **only with your explicit permission**, and never with your name or institution attached without a separate, explicit OK.
- Raw forms are seen only by the kit author and the teaching centre staff coordinating the review.

## What we will not do

- No audio or video recording without asking you first, in the session.
- No use of your comments in marketing or external reporting beyond the kit's evaluation pages.
- No automated analysis of your writing: feedback is read by a person.

## Your rights

- Withdraw at any time — even after submitting — by contacting [Email]; your form is then deleted, no questions asked.
- Ask what data about you exists, at any time.
- Anonymous participation is always available: the forms can be completed with no identifying information at all.

## Contact

Questions, concerns, or to withdraw: [Email] · [LinkedIn]
