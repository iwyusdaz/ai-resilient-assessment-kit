What exists today is (1) the structured **Agent review** — a separate reviewer agent working from the form below, labelled as agent review and subject to a same-model limitation — and (2) the ready-to-run materials for real educator review in [`evaluation/human-protocol/`](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/ai-resilient-assessment-kit/tree/main/evaluation/human-protocol). When ≥3 educators have reviewed, this page will carry the anonymised summary and quotes (with permission), and [revisions](revisions.html) will log the changes they caused.

## Review form (as used by the agent review, and by human reviewers)

Each reviewer takes **one case + the decision tool**, not everything. Estimated time: 45–60 minutes.

### Part 1 — Reviewer profile (optional, anonymous)

- Discipline / role: ______ (e.g., statistics lecturer, TA, faculty developer)
- Years teaching: ______ · Typical class size: ______
- Have you already redesigned an assessment for AI? yes / partly / no

### Part 2 — The case (rate 1–5 + one paragraph each)

1. **Clarity.** Could you follow the stress-test → redesign → rubric chain without asking anyone?
2. **Validity of the redesign.** Does the new assessment certify what the original claimed to certify?
3. **Feasibility.** Would the grading-time and scaling notes survive contact with YOUR timetable? What breaks first?
4. **Equity.** What does this redesign do for (or to) multilingual students, students with accommodations, students without AI access?
5. **Student experience.** Would you defend this design to students in week 1?
6. **Would you use it?** As-is / with changes / no — and what change matters most?

### Part 3 — The decision tool

7. Answer the six questions for one of your own assessments. Were the recommendations and the "Why this recommendation?" reasoning **right**, partly right, or wrong? What would you add to the reasoning?
8. Anything missing from the eight approaches?

### Part 4 — Missing pieces

9. What's missing from this kit for YOUR context (discipline, institution type, country)?

## How the feedback will be used

- Verbatim quotes only with explicit permission (the invitation text asks).
- Every accepted change is logged in [revisions](revisions.html) with the reviewer's point and what changed; rejected feedback is logged too, with the reason.
- Reviewer identities are never attached to published quotes.
