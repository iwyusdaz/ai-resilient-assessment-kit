# Case A — new rubric

5 criteria × 4 levels, 40 points total (scaled to 35% of the course grade across stages). Two criteria are process criteria — they grade the capabilities the [stress test](stress-test.html) showed the original rubric couldn't see.

## The rubric

| Criterion | 10 — Accomplished | 7.5 — Proficient | 5 — Developing | 2.5 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Argument construction** (10) | Thesis is precise and contestable; every section advances it; implications are specific and earned | Clear thesis; argument consistent; implications stated | Thesis present but general; argument drifts or restates | No sustained thesis; summary replaces argument |
| **2. Source verification & evidence integrity** (10) | All five sources verified with access route and page-anchored quotation; annotations show the *specific* content of each source; references flawless | All five verified; annotations accurate; minor reference slips | Some sources verified; one annotation is generic or unverifiable | Sources unverified or missing; annotation pattern suggests the texts weren't read |
| **3. Engagement with counter-arguments** (10) | Strongest version of the counter-case presented, then answered with reasons, not dismissal | Counter-argument engaged and answered | Counter-argument acknowledged; answer is thin | Counter-argument absent or strawmanned |
| **4. AI-use judgment & disclosure** (10) | Log is specific and honest; at least one substantive rejection or verification of AI output, explained; disclosure statement complete | Log honest and complete; judgments mostly explained | Log present but vague; little evidence of critical AI use | No log, or log contradicts the work; undisclosed AI use |
| **5. Clarity & presentation** (10) | Prose carries the argument; signposting, APA 7 consistent throughout | Clear writing; minor APA issues | Readable but uneven; frequent slips | Disorganized; obscures the argument |

**Stage weighting feeds these criteria:** stages 1–2 are graded with checklists against criteria 2 and 4 (their stage marks convert into the course grade separately); the essay and defence are graded on all five criteria.

## Alignment to the course outcome

| Course outcome element | Rubric criterion | Where the evidence comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Select and verify scholarly evidence | 2 (source verification) | Annotated source list + verification notes + defence question (a) |
| Reason from evidence | 1 (argument) + 3 (counter-arguments) | Final essay |
| Defend argumentative choices | 1 + 3 + defence | Defence questions (b) and (c) |
| Evaluate AI use critically (course AI-literacy outcome) | 4 (AI-use judgment) | Draft + AI-use log + disclosure statement |
| Communicate to academic standards | 5 (clarity) | Final essay |

## Why each criterion replaced its predecessor

- **Criterion 1** keeps the original "thesis & argument" intent but raises the specificity bar: "sustained, logically ordered" becomes "every section advances it".
- **Criterion 2** replaces "use of evidence (≥5 sources, APA)" — the criterion the [stress test](stress-test.html) flagged as highest-risk because it rewards citation formatting. It now rewards *verification*, which generic AI output cannot fake without reading the sources.
- **Criterion 3** keeps counter-argument engagement but requires the *strongest version* of the counter-case — raising the floor above generic "some argue…, however…".
- **Criterion 4** is new: it grades the honesty and judgment of AI use. An honest log full of rejected suggestions scores 10; a silent AI-polished essay scores 2.5 — *not* for using AI, but for hiding it.
- **Criterion 5** keeps fluency as a communication criterion but is now the smallest part of the assurance story, since fluency alone is [no longer diagnostic](stress-test.html#vulnerability-analysis-design-reasoning-not-measured-results).

## Gradebook mapping

| Component | Weight | Graded via |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 proposal | 5% | Checklist (complete/revise) |
| Stage 2 annotated sources | 10% | Checklist on criterion 2 elements |
| Stage 3 draft + log | 0% (completion) | Completion check; log informs criterion 4 |
| Stage 4 final essay | 15% | All 5 criteria (scaled to 40 pts → 15%) |
| Stage 5 defence | 5% | Criteria 1–4 in oral/written form (pass/revise/redo per question) |
| **Total** | **35%** | |
