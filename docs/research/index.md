# Research

The evidence layer under the kit's designs: what was checked, how the stress test works, and what its results will (and will not) show.

- **[Literature notes](literature-notes.html)** — every source the kit cites, with access dates and verification status (including the sources that could not be fetched directly).
- **[Stress-test protocol](stress-test-protocol.html)**: the method of record for the AI stress test: the planned full design (three prompt strategies × two models, blind cross-judging) and the smaller agent-simulated run actually carried out on 2026-11-11.

The raw outputs and checks live in the repository (not on this site) under `research/results/`, and `evaluation/METHODS.md` describes every evaluation method, what was run, by what, and what was not run. No fabricated numbers anywhere.
